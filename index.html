Selected work at Merative

The details behind the reports.

These notes describe my contributions and reporting decisions. Client names, source data, and internal deliverables are kept private.

01
Program reporting

Comparing healthcare program cohorts

Participant and nonparticipant comparisons across multiple reporting years.

What I worked on

Prepared financial, utilization, risk, and preventive-care comparisons for a healthcare program refresh. The reporting brief required specific cohort rules, including the exclusion of nonclaimants.

Decisions and checks

Aligned population criteria, claimant exclusions, reporting periods, and measure definitions. Checked that the report outputs and presentation used the same populations and labels.

Deliverable

Updated reports and presentation comparisons. The results described differences between groups; they did not establish that the program caused those differences.

Health Insights · Cohort definitions · Excel · PowerPoint

02
Cost and utilization

Reporting high-cost claimant trends

Employer reporting across two incurred years, including annual cost per member.

What I worked on

Prepared high-cost claimant report suites for defined employer populations and summarized the year-over-year results in executive slides.

Decisions and checks

Applied the correct population subset and incurred period to each report. Checked per-member annual cost calculations, numerical precision, and agreement between the workbook and presentation.

Deliverable

Report workbooks and a concise presentation of claimant and cost trends, with consistent definitions across the reporting package.

Claims reporting · PMPY · Excel · Executive reporting

03
Dashboard migration

Checking definitions during a platform transition

Legacy dashboard documentation, recreation, and validation in a newer reporting environment.

What I worked on

Documented legacy dashboard cards, filters and reference text, and supported dashboard recreation and testing during a reporting-platform migration.

Decisions and checks

Investigated calculated percentages, numerator and denominator definitions, and how top-N and physician-type filters changed totals. Treated a missing benchmark separately from a calculation discrepancy.

Deliverable

Dashboard documentation, recreated reporting elements, and a record of discrepancies. Open calculations and benchmark gaps were raised for further review.

Cognos · Kyvos · Requirements documentation · Dashboard validation

04
Population health

Defining the eligible diabetes population

Condition-based reporting with continuous enrollment and measure-specific eligibility.

What I worked on

Prepared diabetes and impaired-glucose reporting, including condition subgroups and quality measures for eligible member populations.

Decisions and checks

Distinguished diabetes types and applied enrollment criteria consistently. Reviewed the populations and measure selection for HbA1c, eye, and neuropathy reporting, and corrected measure selection during quality review.

Deliverable

Reviewed cohort and quality-report outputs with the eligibility rules documented so the measures could be interpreted in context.

Cohort definitions · Enrollment criteria · Quality measures · QC

How I approach the work

I spend a lot of time making sure the numbers agree.

A changed enrollment rule or denominator can change the story a report tells. I work through those details before turning the output into a dashboard, workbook, or presentation.

My health informatics training helps me connect those reporting choices to healthcare terminology and clinical workflows. I’m interested in analyst roles where I can combine that context with more hands-on SQL, Python, and BI work.
