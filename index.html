Business question: How do defined member groups compare across reporting years and measures?

My contribution: Applied cohort criteria and claimant exclusions, prepared financial and risk reports, and updated the presentation of comparisons.

Analytical focus: Population eligibility, reporting periods, measure definitions, and consistency between reports and slides. These comparisons describe group differences; they do not establish that the program caused those differences.
