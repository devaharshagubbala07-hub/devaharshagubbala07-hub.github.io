      What makes the comparison useful?

      Keep the denominators visible.

      Separate no-shows from cancellations. Sum the selected counts before calculating a rate, so a small team does not receive the same weight as a large one.

      Look beyond the median.

      Wait times use completed visits only. The P90 shows the longer waits that a median can hide; neither measure represents people who never started a visit.

      Ask what to investigate next.

      Compare lead-time groups within each team, inspect short-notice cancellations, and identify what reminder or capacity data would be needed before testing a change.

      Read this as a demonstration.

      The fixture has no clinical outcomes, clinician capacity, reminder history, or rescheduling chains. It cannot establish why a difference occurred or estimate the effect of an intervention. Walk through the analysis and practice explaining it.
