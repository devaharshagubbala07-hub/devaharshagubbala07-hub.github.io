      The denominator matters

      PMPM divides accepted allowed cost by enrollment months, including zero-claim members. Plan averages are weighted by member-months.

      The threshold is illustrative

      High-cost membership is recalculated for the selected year and plan. Partial-year costs are not annualized.

      Comparisons are descriptive

      Changing membership and random service use affect the result. This example does not estimate program savings or establish causes.
